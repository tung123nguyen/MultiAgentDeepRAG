## How did Amazon's different business segments (AWS, Advertising, Third-party seller services, and Online stores) perform in 2023 and 2024?

### Focused Query 1: Amazon AWS revenue and operating income 2023 2024
AWS continued to be a significant growth and profit engine for Amazon.
- **2023:** Revenue was $90.76 billion with an operating income of $24.63 billion.
- **2024:** Revenue grew to $107.56 billion, representing an approximately 18.5% year-over-year increase. Operating income surged to $39.83 billion, showing significant margin expansion.
- **Quarterly Trend (2024):** 
  - Q1: $25.04B revenue
  - Q2: $26.28B revenue (19% YoY growth)
  - Q3: $27.45B revenue (19% YoY growth)
  - Operating income for the first nine months of 2024 was $29.20B compared to $17.46B in the same period of 2023.

### Focused Query 2: Amazon Advertising services revenue 2023 2024
Advertising services remained one of Amazon's fastest-growing revenue streams.
- **2023:** Revenue was $46.91 billion.
- **2024:** Revenue increased to $56.21 billion, a growth of nearly 20% YoY.
- **Quarterly Trend (2024):**
  - Q1: $11.82B
  - Q2: $12.77B
  - Q3: $14.33B

### Focused Query 3: Amazon Third-party seller services and Online stores revenue 2023 2024
The core retail-related services showed steady performance and growth.
- **Third-party seller services:**
  - 2023: $140.05 billion.
  - 2024: $156.15 billion.
  - This segment includes commissions, fulfillment, and shipping fees.
- **Online stores:**
  - 2023: $231.87 billion.
  - 2024: $247.03 billion.
  - Growth in online stores was more moderate compared to service-based segments but remained the largest single revenue contributor.

### Summary
Amazon's performance in 2023 and 2024 was characterized by robust growth in high-margin service segments. AWS and Advertising were standout performers, with AWS seeing a massive jump in operating profitability in 2024. The Third-party seller services segment continued to outpace the growth of Amazon's own Online stores, reflecting the company's shift towards being a service provider for other merchants. Notably, the International segment also returned to profitability in 2024, contributing to the overall consolidated operating income growth from $36.85B in 2023 to $68.59B in 2024.
