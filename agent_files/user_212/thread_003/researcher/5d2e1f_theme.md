## How did Amazon's different business segments (AWS, Advertising, Third-party seller services, and Online stores) perform in 2023 and 2024?

### Focused Query 1: Amazon segment revenue AWS advertising third-party seller services 2023
In 2023, Amazon saw growth across all its primary service-based segments, which continued to outpace the growth of its traditional retail business.
- **AWS:** Generated $90.8 billion in revenue, a 13% increase year-over-year. It remained a critical driver of the company's overall operating income.
- **Third-party Seller Services:** This segment grew significantly to $140.1 billion, up from $117.7 billion in 2022. It includes commissions and fulfillment/shipping fees from the marketplace.
- **Advertising Services:** One of the fastest-growing segments, reaching $46.9 billion in 2023, compared to $37.7 billion in 2022.
- **Online Stores:** Revenue reached $231.9 billion, a modest increase from $220.0 billion in 2022, reflecting a shift toward service-based revenue.

### Focused Query 2: Amazon Q1 2024 segment performance AWS advertising
The first quarter of 2024 showed an acceleration in high-margin segments.
- **AWS:** Revenue was $25.0 billion, a 17% year-over-year increase. Operating income for the segment nearly doubled to $9.4 billion.
- **North America & International:** Both segments benefited from "increased unit sales, including sales by third-party sellers, advertising sales, and subscription services."
- **Note:** Advertising continued to be a major contributor to the profitability of the retail segments.

### Focused Query 3: Amazon Q2 2024 segment performance AWS advertising third-party seller services
Growth in the service segments remained robust through the second quarter of 2024.
- **AWS:** Revenue reached $26.3 billion, representing a 19% year-over-year growth rate. This shows an acceleration from both 2023 (13%) and Q1 2024 (17%).
- **H1 2024 AWS Totals:** For the first six months of 2024, AWS revenue was $51.3 billion with an operating income of $18.8 billion.
- **Consolidated Net Sales Mix:** By Q2 2024, AWS accounted for 18% of total net sales, while North America and International accounted for 61% and 21% respectively.

### Summary
Amazon's financial performance in 2023 and 2024 is increasingly defined by its service-oriented segments. While **Online Stores** remains the largest single revenue source ($231.9B in 2023), the growth and profitability are being driven by **AWS** (which reached 19% YoY growth by Q2 2024), **Advertising Services** ($46.9B in 2023), and **Third-party Seller Services** ($140.1B in 2023). AWS, in particular, has seen a re-acceleration in growth and a massive surge in operating income, contributing over 60% of the company's total operating income in the first half of 2024.
