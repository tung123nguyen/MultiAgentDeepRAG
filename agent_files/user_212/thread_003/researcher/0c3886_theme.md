## [THEME 1] What were Amazon's key financial highlights and revenue growth in 2023 and the first half of 2024?

### Focused Query 1: Amazon full year 2023 financial results revenue net income
- **Total Net Sales:** In 2023, Amazon's total net sales reached **$574.8 billion**, a 12% increase compared to $514.0 billion in 2022.
- **Operating Income:** Operating income saw a significant jump to **$36.9 billion** in 2023, compared to $12.2 billion in the previous year.
- **Net Income:** The company reported a net income of **$30.4 billion** ($2.90 per diluted share) for 2023, a major recovery from the net loss of $2.7 billion ($0.27 per diluted share) in 2022.
- **Segment Breakdown:**
  - **North America:** Sales increased 12% to $352.8 billion.
  - **International:** Sales increased 11% to $131.2 billion.
  - **AWS:** Sales increased 13% to $90.8 billion.

### Focused Query 2: Amazon Q1 2024 revenue and net income
- **Total Net Sales:** Net sales increased 13% to **$143.3 billion** in Q1 2024, compared with $127.4 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Operating Income:** Operating income increased to **$15.3 billion** in Q1 2024, compared with $4.8 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Net Income:** Net income was **$10.4 billion** ($0.98 per diluted share), up from $3.2 billion ($0.31 per diluted share) in the prior-year period.
- **Accounting Change:** Effective January 1, 2024, Amazon increased the estimated useful life of its servers from five to six years. This change reduced depreciation expense by $0.9 billion and increased net income by $0.7 billion in Q1 2024.

### Focused Query 3: Amazon Q2 2024 revenue and net income
- **Total Net Sales:** Net sales increased 10% to **$148.0 billion** in Q2 2024, compared with $134.4 billion in Q2 2023.
- **Operating Income:** Operating income was **$14.7 billion** in Q2 2024, compared with $7.7 billion in Q2 2023.
- **Net Income:** Net income increased to **$13.5 billion** ($1.26 per diluted share) in Q2 2024, compared with $6.8 billion ($0.65 per diluted share) in Q2 2023.
- **First Half 2024 Summary:** For the six months ended June 30, 2024, total net sales were **$291.3 billion**, operating income was **$30.0 billion**, and net income reached **$23.9 billion**.

### Focused Query 4: Amazon 2023 vs 2022 revenue growth comparison
- **Consolidated Growth:** 12% growth in 2023 (12% excluding foreign exchange) vs 9% growth in 2022 (13% excluding foreign exchange).
- **North America Growth:** Remained steady at 12% in 2023 (same as 2022).
- **International Growth:** Recovered significantly to 11% growth in 2023, compared to an 8% decline in 2022.
- **AWS Growth:** Decelerated to 13% growth in 2023, down from 29% in 2022, reflecting customer cost optimization efforts. However, AWS growth began to re-accelerate in early 2024 (17% in Q1 and 19% in Q2).

### Summary
Amazon's financial performance in 2023 and the first half of 2024 is characterized by robust revenue growth and a dramatic improvement in profitability. After a net loss in 2022, the company achieved over $30 billion in net income in 2023 and nearly $24 billion in just the first six months of 2024. This turnaround was driven by strong performance across all segments, particularly a recovery in the International segment and significant growth in operating income in North America. AWS, while seeing slower growth in 2023 compared to 2022, showed signs of re-acceleration in 2024. Profitability was also bolstered by an accounting change regarding server useful lives and continued focus on operational efficiencies.
