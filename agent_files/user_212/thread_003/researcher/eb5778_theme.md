## What were Amazon's key financial highlights and revenue growth in 2023 and the first half of 2024?

### Focused Query 1: Amazon full year 2023 revenue and net income
- **Total Net Sales**: $574.8 billion, a 12% increase from $514.0 billion in 2022.
- **Operating Income**: $36.9 billion, a significant increase from $12.2 billion in 2022.
- **Net Income**: $30.4 billion (or $2.90 per diluted share), compared to a net loss of $2.7 billion in 2022.
- **Segment Performance**:
    - North America: $352.8 billion (up 12% YoY).
    - International: $131.2 billion (up 11% YoY).
    - AWS: $90.8 billion (up 13% YoY).

### Focused Query 2: Amazon Q1 2024 revenue and net income
- **Total Net Sales**: $143.3 billion, a 13% increase compared to $127.4 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Operating Income**: $15.3 billion, compared to $4.8 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Net Income**: $10.4 billion (or $0.98 per diluted share), compared to $3.2 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Key Drivers**: Increased unit sales (specifically third-party sellers), advertising sales growth, and AWS customer usage growth.

### Focused Query 3: Amazon Q2 2024 revenue and net income
- **Total Net Sales**: $148.0 billion, a 10% increase compared to $134.4 billion in Q2 2023.
- **Operating Income**: $14.7 billion, compared to $7.7 billion in Q2 2023.
- **Net Income**: $13.5 billion (or $1.26 per diluted share), compared to $6.8 billion in Q2 2023.
- **AWS Performance**: AWS sales increased 19% YoY to $26.3 billion, showing accelerating growth compared to previous quarters.

### Summary
Amazon's financial performance in 2023 and the first half of 2024 has been characterized by robust revenue growth and a dramatic improvement in profitability. Total net sales reached $574.8 billion in 2023 (12% growth) and continued strong in H1 2024, reaching $291.3 billion (11% growth). The most notable trend is the surge in operating income, which more than tripled in 2023 compared to 2022 and nearly doubled in Q1 and Q2 2024 relative to the previous year's quarters. This profitability has been driven by efficiencies in the fulfillment network, strong performance in the high-margin Advertising and AWS segments, and disciplined cost management across the board.
