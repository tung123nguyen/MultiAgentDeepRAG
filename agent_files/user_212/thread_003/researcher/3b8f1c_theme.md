## What were Amazon's key financial highlights and revenue growth in 2023 and the first half of 2024?

### Focused Query 1: Amazon 2023 annual revenue and net income highlights
Amazon demonstrated a significant financial recovery in 2023 compared to 2022.
- **Total Net Sales:** $574.8 billion, representing a 12% increase from $514.0 billion in 2022.
- **Operating Income:** $36.9 billion, a substantial increase from $12.2 billion in 2022.
- **Net Income:** $30.4 billion ($2.90 per diluted share), compared to a net loss of $2.7 billion ($0.27 per diluted share) in 2022.
- **Sales Mix:** Net product sales reached $255.9 billion, while net service sales grew to $318.9 billion.

### Focused Query 2: Amazon Q1 2024 financial results revenue growth
The positive momentum continued into the first quarter of 2024.
- **Net Sales:** $143.3 billion, up 13% year-over-year.
- **Operating Income:** Increased to $15.3 billion, compared with $4.8 billion in Q1 2023.
- **Segment Performance:**
  - North America: $86.3 billion (up 12% YoY).
  - International: $31.9 billion (up 10% YoY).
  - AWS: $25.0 billion (up 17% YoY).
- **Key Factor:** Operating income benefited from a change in the estimated useful life of servers (from 5 to 6 years), which reduced depreciation expense by $897 million and boosted net income by $695 million ($0.07 per diluted share).

### Focused Query 3: Amazon Q2 2024 financial results revenue growth
Amazon's performance remained strong in Q2 2024, particularly in the AWS segment.
- **Net Sales:** $148.0 billion, up 10% year-over-year.
- **Operating Income:** $14.7 billion, up from $7.7 billion in Q2 2023.
- **Segment Performance:**
  - North America: $90.0 billion (up 9% YoY).
  - International: $31.7 billion (up 7% YoY).
  - AWS: $26.3 billion (up 19% YoY).
- **First Half 2024 Summary:** For the first six months of 2024, total net sales reached $291.3 billion (up 11% YoY), and operating income totaled $30.0 billion (up from $12.5 billion in H1 2023).

### Summary
In 2023, Amazon achieved a major turnaround with $574.8 billion in sales and $30.4 billion in net income, following a loss in 2022. This growth trend accelerated in the first half of 2024, reaching $291.3 billion in revenue and $30.0 billion in operating income. A notable driver of profitability in 2024 was the increased efficiency of AWS and the accounting change extending the useful life of servers, which provided a significant boost to net income.
